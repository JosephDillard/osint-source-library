Code examples embedded in documentation remain available under MIT even when the surrounding prose uses CC BY 4.0. Generated source descriptions and library pages follow the content license; running a generator does not change the license of the source material.

The content license grants only rights the contributors hold. It does not create ownership of facts, individual URLs, public-domain material, provider names or third-party page titles. It does not impose restrictions where no licensed permission is required. Preserve source-specific attribution and terms when separately using provider material.

## Reusing catalog content and documentation

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits sharing and adaptation, including commercially. When sharing covered material, provide appropriate attribution, retain supplied notices, identify modifications and include the license text or a link to it. The full terms, including the warranty disclaimer, are in [LICENSE-CONTENT](LICENSE-CONTENT).

Suggested attribution for original project material:

> OSINT Source Library by Joseph Dillard and contributors, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Source: https://github.com/JosephDillard/osint-source-library. Changes: describe your modifications, or state that the material is unmodified.

Link to the specific source file and revision when practical. Attribution must not imply that the project or a data provider endorses your work. The suggested wording is an example; the license governs acceptable attribution.

## Contributions and third-party material

Contribute original code under MIT and original catalog content or prose under CC BY 4.0, following the scope above. Retain applicable notices and clearly identify third-party material; do not claim rights you cannot grant. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Public access to a source does not itself grant permission to redistribute its data or integrate its API. A catalog entry's provider-license notes describe that provider's terms, not the license of this repository's original annotation.
